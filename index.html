What your options add up to in stock

Each option moves like some number of shares (its delta × 100 × contracts). Calls act like owning shares, puts like being short shares. Added up, this is the stock position your options behave like right now.

Lost so far

What you paid minus what the options are worth: now, and if the stock were at each pair's midpoint right now (each option priced to match its live market price). TV = time value (price − intrinsic value), from your fills.
